Kenney — Pixel Platformer (v1.2)
https://kenney.nl/assets/pixel-platformer

Selected atlas and both sample Tiled layouts are released under Creative
Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The map layouts are based on the pack's Sample A and Sample B Tiled maps.
They are stored as JSON tile layers for the game's canvas renderer. Character
layers were omitted because the race scene supplies its own runners.
